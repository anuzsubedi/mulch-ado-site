Mulch Ado Support

Need help?

Questions, bug reports or ideas are welcome. Open a request at github.com/anuzsubedi/mulch-ado-site/issues and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases? Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom.

What does No Ads do? It removes every ad. Rewards that normally need an ad (Revive, Rain, Weed killer and more) are given straight away.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your garden progress; purchases can always be restored.
