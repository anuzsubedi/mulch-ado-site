Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Mulch Ado: Merge Garden ("the game"), made by Anuz Subedi, handles information.

What the game stores

Your progress (scores, coins, unlocked themes, settings) is stored only on your device. We don't run our own servers and we don't collect your name, email or contacts.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one, and an occasional ad may play between games. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions and app performance in order to show, measure and limit ads. Where required, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and on iPhone you can decline tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying No Ads turns off all ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive only a confirmation of what was bought; we never see your payment details.

Leaderboards and achievements

If you're signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your scores and achievements are sent to that service and shown with your player name, under Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings, decline the consent prompt, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: mulchado@quivren.com.

Changes

We'll update this page if the policy changes.
